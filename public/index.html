Built to send fewer messages, not more.

  • You approve every message. Nothing reaches a customer until someone on your team says yes.
  • One link per failed payment. No reminder chains, no second nudge.
  • Consent first. Customers who have not agreed to be contacted get nothing.
  • Risky cases wait. Possible duplicate charges, disputes and unclear failures go to a person.
  • A record that can't be quietly edited. Every approval carries the approver's name in a hash-chained log.
  • Test mode first. Live payments only after you switch them on yourself.

Questions stores ask

Will my customers feel spammed?

No. Each failed payment gets at most one link, and only after you approve it. Customers who have not agreed to be contacted get nothing.

What does the customer receive?

A standard Razorpay payment link by SMS and email, sent from your Razorpay account with your business name. They pay the way they tried to before.

Does RecoverFlow touch my money?

No. Customers pay you through Razorpay and it settles to your account as usual. RecoverFlow only sees payment events, and sends an invoice for 8% once a month.

What if the customer already paid another way?

Close the case without contacting them. Nothing is sent and nothing is charged.

Does it work with Shopify or WooCommerce?

Yes, if your checkout uses Razorpay. RecoverFlow sees every payment that reached Razorpay and failed. Carts abandoned before the payment step are not visible to it yet.

What does “hear it” mean?

The dashboard can announce recoveries out loud, like a counter speaker announces payments received. It's off until you turn it on.
