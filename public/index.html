Questions stores ask

What does the customer receive?

A standard Razorpay payment link by SMS and email, from your Razorpay account with your business name.

Does RecoverFlow touch my money?

No. Customers pay you through Razorpay and it settles to your account as usual. RecoverFlow invoices 8% once a month.

What if the customer already paid another way?

Close the case without contacting them. Nothing is sent and nothing is charged.

Does it work with Shopify or WooCommerce?

Yes, if your checkout uses Razorpay. It sees every payment that reached Razorpay and failed.
