Non-negotiable guardrails

What the agent will never do

One retry per 24 hours

A second failure is held for a human. Repeated retries erode trust and trigger issuer blocks.

retry_limit_reached

No contact after opt-out

Consent is checked before any message is drafted. No consent, no outreach.

NO_CONSENT

No unapproved money action

Retries, refunds and payments always wait for explicit human approval.

HUMAN_GATE

No hidden exceptions

Duplicates, disputes and low-confidence cases stay visible in the queue.
